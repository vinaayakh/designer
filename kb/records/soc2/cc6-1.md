## Requirement

*In our own words; AICPA text is not reproduced.*

The organisation puts technical access controls around the systems and data in scope. Everyone and everything that touches them (people, services, devices) is identified and authenticated. Access is granted by role and kept to what each needs. Sensitive systems sit in separate network zones. Data is encrypted where exposure would hurt. Access rules live in managed infrastructure (identity provider, gateways, firewalls), not scattered through application code.

## How to check on a diagram

- An identity provider or auth service sits in front of user-facing entry points; service-to-service edges show an auth mechanism.
- Data stores and back-end compute sit in private Groups (private subnet / VNet), reachable only through a gateway, load balancer or bastion.
- Stores holding PII, PHI or confidential data are encrypted at rest, with key management present.
- Not visible on a diagram: role design, access reviews, credential policies. Report these as outside the diagram.

## Suggested fixes

- Add an identity provider in front of public entry points.
- Move data stores into a private Group with a single controlled ingress.
- Encrypt classified data stores and add key management.
